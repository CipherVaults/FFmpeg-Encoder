# Logo candidates — October 29, 2026 (owner selection pending)

Owner-requested R07 candidate set. **Not shipped:** the app still uses the Folded Frame icon in `assets/app-icon/` until Ahmed selects. Each concept folder has its SVG master, hand-tuned 16/24/32 px optical SVGs, rendered PNGs, a preview sheet on dark and light grounds, `concept.md` (meaning, palette, construction, originality statement) and a `build.cjs` that rebuilds only inside its folder. `logo-comparison-2026-10-29.png` shows all three beside the current icon.

All are original static geometry (no text, fonts, filters, scripts or raster) and avoid the FFmpeg, Electron and Chromium marks. No trademark search was performed.

| Concept | Idea | Judges' view |
|---|---|---|
| A — Precision Aperture | Ivory aperture ring with an amber "quality choice" segment and a teal centre | Ranked last by all three: reads as a camera/lens/scan pictogram and its arc as a progress spinner; best 16 px silhouette |
| B — Kept Frame | Intact ivory source frame, separated by an ink gap from an amber→teal bracket that folds into the output | Ranked first by two judges: most app-specific meaning (source protected), lowest trademark exposure, best continuity with Folded Frame and the UI's rectilinear panels |
| C — Kept Streams | Stacked warm stream bars (video/audio/subtitles) carried through one step, with a subtle tile rim | Ranked first by one judge and second by two: most attractive and memorable, its rim fixes dark-taskbar edge loss; less tied to the app's panel style |

**Judges' recommended refinements before the final choice:** for B, borrow C's 1–4 px tile rim (about #2B3B44–#33464B) so the tile reads on dark taskbars, enlarge the crease so it reads as a fold rather than a document dog-ear, and darken the crease colour for contrast against teal. For C, tighten small-size legibility. A real Windows taskbar/title-bar check and the ICO build follow selection.

Owner decision needed: pick B, C, a refined B, or keep Folded Frame.
