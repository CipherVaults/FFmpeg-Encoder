# Concept C: Kept Streams

Concept id: `concept-c-layered-stream`. This is an unaccepted icon candidate for owner review, created on October 30, 2026. It does not replace `assets/app-icon/app-icon.svg` and nothing in the app references it.

## Meaning

The mark shows three media streams stacked as parallel bands: amber for the picture, jade teal for the sound and warm ivory for the subtitles. Subtitles are the thinnest band, and ivory is the colour of caption text. Together the bands climb one 45-degree step. That step stands for an encode or remux pass. All three bands follow the same centerline with true parallel offsets, so they turn at the same moment, keep the same widths and keep the same spacing before, during and after the step. Nothing is dropped, merged or reordered. All three bands also start and stop on the same two vertical cuts, so they begin together and finish together. That is how the mark shows streams kept in sync and preserved through a long job. The dark ink tile is the calm workbench that holds them. The bands never touch its edge.

## Construction (256 master, `icon.svg`)

- Tile: `rect 8,8 240x240 rx52` (the same footprint as the current icon), plus a 4px even-odd rim ring. The area outside the tile is transparent.
- Streams, top to bottom: 30, 22 and 14 units wide, with 11-unit gaps, for a bundle 88 units thick. The bundle is centred on the tile's centre.
- Centerline: runs east, has a 45-degree vertex at (104, 152), rises J = 48 to (152, 104), then runs east again. Each bend is a concentric arc around a centerline radius of R = 56, so the innermost edge radius is 12 and the outermost is 100. Every edge is an exact parallel offset.
- Ends: shared vertical cuts at x = 50 and x = 206, with a 6-unit corner radius. The bounding box is x 50–206 and y 60–196, which is optically centred.
- Static SVG only: one `rect` and four `path` elements, filled flat colours, `fill-rule="evenodd"` for the rim, plus `title` and `desc` metadata. It has no scripts, links, `use`, `style`, `url()`, gradients, filters, masks, fonts, text elements or raster images. `build.cjs` asserts this for every SVG it writes.

## Small optical sizes (hand-tuned, whole-pixel)

| Size | Tile | Streams (video/gap/audio/gap/text) | Step | Method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 1,1 14x14 rx3 + 1px rim | 2 / 1 / 2 / 1 / 1 px | rise 3, columns 7–9 | 1px-per-column staircase. The 1px ivory band gets a one-row foot on its rising columns so its diagonal stays connected rather than dotted |
| 24 | 1,1 22x22 rx5 + 1px rim | 3 / 1 / 2 / 1 / 1 px | rise 5, columns 10–14 | Same staircase and foot |
| 32 | 1,1 30x30 rx7 + 1px rim | 4 / 2 / 3 / 2 / 2 px | rise 6, x 13–19 | Straight 45-degree run between integer vertices, with 2.5px quadratic bends that echo the master's curves. The 2px gaps leave one fully clear pixel per column on the diagonal |

How the small sizes are simplified:

- At 16 and 24 px, the curves become pixel staircases. On a 45-degree vector edge, a 1px gap would blur away, and the streams would merge (this happened in early tests).
- Corner radii on the band ends are dropped, but the shared flat cuts are kept.
- Stream widths are rounded to whole pixels, keeping the order video ≥ audio ≥ text.
- Bundle extents are centred on the tile. At 24 and 32 px, the mark sits half a pixel low, which counters the heavier amber at the top.
- 48 and 256 are downsampled from the master.

## Rendering

- `node build.cjs`, run from any folder. It writes only into this folder.
- The master is rendered by ImageMagick 7.1.1-47 (librsvg) at an integer supersample: 4x for 256 and 16x for 48. It is then box-averaged, which gives true area coverage without the dark Lanczos ringing next to the bright bands.
- The optical SVGs are rendered at 8x and box-averaged.
- A one-level alpha fringe is cleared, as the existing generator does. All PNGs are 8-bit RGBA sRGB.
- `preview-sheet.png` shows the following, all generated by the script:
  - 16, 24, 32, 48 and 256 px at actual pixels on the dark `#101318` and light `#F3F5F7` surfaces.
  - Windows-style dark `#202020` and light `#E9ECEF` taskbar strips at 24 and 32 px.
  - 8x nearest-neighbour views of 16, 24 and 32 px, and a 4x view of 48 px.

## Known limits

- Some viewers may read three parallel stepped stripes as a generic "data flow", "growth" or "stairs" motif rather than specifically "video". The media meaning relies on the stream colours and the app context.
- At 16 px, adjacent streams touch corner-to-corner on the three rising columns, so the step looks slightly checkered when magnified. It reads as a step at actual size.
- The rim's lighter edge separates the tile on dark taskbars. On light taskbars, it shows as a faint bevel.
- The image has only been reviewed as rendered PNGs. Windows shell, taskbar, titlebar and ICO behaviour have not been checked, and no owner visual acceptance has been given.
