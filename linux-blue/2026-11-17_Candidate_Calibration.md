## Implementation and review

The runtime adapter privately brands a verified exact pair and an explicit bounded dependency list. It snapshots inert argv/options, fixes executable selection, checks pair/dependencies around owned calls and preserves normal child ownership. Copied/closed handles and arbitrary public shapes cannot grant candidate authority. It is only a main-owned developer seam; no CLI/environment/renderer/IPC activation exists. CPU guards are not a general sandbox, and the explicit dependency list is not discovered full loader closure.

The Keep720 candidate campaign uses its final telemetry-enabled plan to obtain a genuine family selection. Actual source identity and current runtime are checked; exact issued budget, timing and policy references reach the collector. Frozen preregistered clock bounds and independent resource predictions must agree with the issued resources. Long/late/open-ended and held-out source cases, storage/child/deadline limits and false publication/catalog flags remain intact. Other historical campaign entry points and unsupported families are not activated.

Cancellation invalidates the original token. A temporary fresh observation checks settlement without reviving it. A failed child plus changed dependency retains both causes. Independent review found that the campaign report dropped the secondary error; a bounded own-data serializer and campaign-level regression now preserve it. The added diagnostic caps code at 128 characters and message at 2,048 and does not traverse recursive graphs or invoke getters for those fields.
