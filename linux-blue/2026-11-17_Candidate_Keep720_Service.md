## 6. Problems Encountered

The cancellation and dual-cleanup defects were found before committing or running real service media and corrected in v2. Admission closure is not equivalent to revoking every externally retained candidate token: idle/prepared cancellation can leave the token current until resource closure. Required recovery choreography is **cancel → drain → close operation resources → new verified candidate handle and new service**. Cancelled runtime tokens are never revived; same-service recovery remains unproven.

The historical re-entry checker still expects `codex/v0.7-candidate`, direct local/remote identity and an unavailable protected baseline. It reported those three profile failures while the index was empty, no Planning staged and 43 requirement rows present. They were investigated as known reconstruction/profile differences, not fixed by resetting a baseline or editing protected records. The phase-close pre-stage checker also reported its historical branch mismatch and pre-existing `.codex/hooks.json` toolkit drift. Those excluded executor copies were not altered or synchronized in this app-code checkpoint. The actual branch/history/explicit scope and source receipts are verified separately. The broader Linux suite's historical failures remain visible.

Source restoration alone cannot make this live factory pass: original raw calibration bytes, candidate binaries and dependency context are deliberately outside the source backup. Requalification or a separately reviewed evidence/runtime restoration would be needed after losing them. No fallback to the portable archival report or historical factory pins is permitted.
